## Our editorial statement

> Advertising on [SITE NAME] never affects ratings, awards, rankings, or which
> restaurants we list or remove. Star ratings come from the third-party
> sources shown beside them and from our users. Awards are recorded from
> published results, with a link to the original source. Sponsored placements
> are labeled 'Sponsored' and appear outside the organic order of results.
> Advertisers cannot review, edit, or remove user reviews, and they cannot pay
> to change the facts on a listing. We accept corrections from owners and
> publish them when verified.

## How we keep advertising separate

- The people who sell advertising do not decide or change ratings, rankings,
  awards, highlights, listings, or review moderation, and editorial staff are
  not paid commission on ad sales.
- Our advertising contracts say that buying an ad does not affect any of
  these. Anyone who offers otherwise on our behalf is breaking this policy.
- A restaurant that buys a sponsored placement still appears in its normal
  position in our regular results, with no boost.
- We never use "Featured," "Top pick," or "Recommended" for a paid placement.
- Awards are never part of an advertising package or sponsor offer, and we do
  not use awards programs' names, logos, or badges in ads.
- Highlights (owner quotes, editor's notes, and reviews) are free. They cannot
  be bought and are not part of any package.
- Links that may earn us a commission are labeled next to the link.
- Text or photos supplied by a restaurant are labeled "Provided by the
  restaurant."

## Moderation rules

Every highlight, review, and owner response waits for an administrator before
it appears. We publish positive and critical reviews alike. We reject
something only when its content breaks one of these rules, and we apply them
the same way whatever the rating:

1. **Spam:** advertising, links to other sites, or the same text posted again
   and again.
2. **Profanity** or obscene language.
3. **Personal attack:** insults aimed at a person (staff, the owner, or
   another reviewer) instead of describing the visit.
4. **Hate speech,** threats, or harassment.
5. **Personal information** about someone, such as a phone number or home
   address.
6. **Conflict of interest:** written by someone connected to the restaurant
   (owner, staff, relative) or to a competitor without saying so.
7. **Fake:** clear signs of being fake, paid for, or written in return for a
   discount or gift.
8. **Off topic:** not about this restaurant or a visit to it.
9. **Wrong business:** about a different business.

In addition:

- We never remove or hide a review because a restaurant, advertiser, or
  prospective advertiser asks, or because it is negative.
- Owners may post one public response to each review, labeled as a response
  from the owner. Owners cannot edit, hide, or delete reviews.
- People linked to a restaurant's account cannot review it, and nobody
  moderates reviews of a restaurant they are connected to.
- Each moderation decision records who made it, when, and which rule applied.
- We never pay for reviews or reward reviewers for what they say.
- Ratings and reviews from other services (such as Google) are shown exactly
  as those services provide them, with the source named. We do not edit,
  filter, or reorder them. Owners who disagree with one are directed to that
  service's own reporting process.

## Corrections

Anyone can report a factual error through the "Suggest a correction" page.
Owners who have claimed their listing can also submit corrections from their
account.

1. **Receive.** Every request is logged privately with the date, who asked,
   what they asked for, the decision, and the reason.
2. **Verify.** We check the fact against a source: the restaurant's own
   website or signage, a government record, a published award result, or
   direct confirmation from the restaurant's verified owner account.
3. **Publish.** Verified corrections to facts (hours, phone, address, menu
   link, cuisine, closed or moved) are published, usually within 2 business
   days, and appear in the listing's change history with the date and source.
4. **Closed restaurants** are marked "Closed" with the date rather than
   deleted, so award history stays accurate.
5. **Our own errors.** When we get a fact wrong in our own writing, we fix it
   and add a dated correction note if the error was significant.

What we do not change on request:

- Ratings, reviews, rankings, or award results. Award results change only if
  the publisher of the award corrects its own published results, and we link
  to that correction.
- Third-party reviews, which we cannot edit.
- A truthful listing of a restaurant that is open to the public. We will
  remove personal information, such as a home caterer's home address or a
  personal phone number.

Requests tied to advertising ("I'll advertise if you change this") are
declined in writing and recorded. Legal notices go to counsel. Photo or text
that an owner says is theirs is handled as a copyright notice
([legal-and-policy.md](legal-and-policy.md) §6).

## Sponsored features

A sponsored feature is an article about one restaurant, event, or business
that the business paid for ([monetization.md](monetization.md), product 9).

**Who writes them.** Our staff write every sponsored feature, in our own
words. Advertisers do not write them. The advertiser may supply facts,
photos, and quotes, and may review a draft to check facts only; they cannot
add claims we cannot verify or require us to remove accurate information.
Staff who write a restaurant's editor's note do not write a sponsored feature
about the same restaurant.

**How they are labeled.**

- The label "Sponsored" appears above the headline and on the lead image, on
  every device, and stays on the page when it is shared or reached from
  search.
- A line under the headline says who paid for it: "Paid for by [business
  name]."
- Sponsored features have their own visual style (border or shading) and are
  never formatted as an editor's note, a "best of" list, or an award.
- Links to the advertiser use `rel="sponsored"`.

**What they never contain.** Star ratings or scores, rankings, comparisons
with named competitors, claims to be "the best," or any awards program's
name, logo, or badge. A sponsored feature does not change the restaurant's
ratings, its place in any list or search, or its highlights.

Whether the label should read "Sponsored" or "Sponsored · Ad" is open with
counsel (question 1 in [legal-and-policy.md](legal-and-policy.md) §8).
