# Market rates log

Last updated 2026-10-10.

Benchmarks and real quotes behind the rate card in
[monetization.md](monetization.md). Add a row whenever a rate card or quote
comes in, with the date, and never delete old rows: the history is what shows
whether prices are drifting.

## Repricing rule

Reprice the rate card in [monetization.md](monetization.md):

1. **90 days after launch**, using the site's actual impressions and
   click-through rates (from `gr_ad_daily_stats` and analytics).
2. **Whenever a Guam quote below contradicts a launch price by more than 25
   percent** for a comparable product, in either direction.

Record each repricing at the bottom of this file: the date, which prices
changed, and the numbers that justified it.

## Benchmarks

Each figure was checked against its source on 2026-10-10. "Checked" means the
page states the figure. Where it does not, the row says what the page does say.
