# Awards research: Pika's Best of Guam

Research date: 2026-10-09. Prepared for the Guam restaurant directory project.

This file records **facts with source URLs** only. It does not copy the
publisher's editorial text, images, or winner lists. Category names below
are recorded as facts so the directory can design its awards data model.
Winner names are not reproduced here; they should be entered only after
the permission/partnership question in the last section is settled (see
CLAUDE.md rule 3).

## 1. What the awards are

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Name | "Pika's Best of Guam" | https://guampdn.biz/pikasbestofguam/2023-Winners.pdf |
| Publisher | Pacific Daily News (PDN), Hagåtña, Guam | same PDF; https://www.guampdn.biz/advertising/pikabestofguam/ |
| What "Pika" refers to | PDN's weekly Friday entertainment section, "GuamPika". It is **not** Pika's Café in Tamuning, which is an unrelated restaurant | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Daily_News |

| Scale | 357,235 total votes reported for the 2023 contest | 2023 winners PDF |
| Placings published | Winner plus 2nd- and 3rd-place finalists per category; a separate "Top 5" nominee list is published before voting | 2023 winners PDF; 2023 media files on guampdn.biz (see §4) |
| Multi-year recognition | The 2023 PDF has a block grouping businesses by consecutive years won (10, 9, 8 … consecutive years) | 2023 winners PDF |

## 2. Year it began

**2014.** The 2023 winners PDF states that the contest started in 2014
and was 10 years old at that point. No independent second source for the
start year was found (see gaps). If the contest has run every year since,
2014–2026 is 13 editions; this has **not** been verified year by year.

## 3. Publication cycle

The cycle is annual and runs in the second half of the calendar year. The
contest is named for the year it runs in (the 2026 vote opened in August
2026).

| Edition | Nominations | Top-5 announced | Voting | Winners announced | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | not found | not found (Top-5 insert files dated Oct 11, 2023) | Oct 20 – Nov 15, 2023 | not found | https://www.guampdn.biz/advertising/pikabestofguam/ |
| 2024 | Aug 27 – Sep 20, 2024 | Sep 24, 2024 | Oct 17 – Nov 8, 2024 | Nov 29, 2024 | schedule printed in the 2023 winners PDF |
| 2026 | not found | not found | opened ~Aug 23, 2026 (PDN post dated Aug 22, 2026 UTC said "opens tomorrow") | not found | https://x.com/GuamPDN/status/2091300633890693318 (search snippet only; X disallows fetching, and the date is decoded from the post ID) |

Typical pattern: nominations late Aug–Sep, top-5 list late Sep or Oct,
voting Oct–Nov, winners late Nov. A printed multi-page insert runs in PDN
(the 2023 page carries 8-page insert images).

## 9. Implications for the directory

- Store awards as facts: source = "Pika's Best of Guam (PDN)", year,
  category, placing (winner / 2nd / 3rd / top 5 nominee), and source URL.
  Do not import winner lists wholesale until PDN replies (CLAUDE.md rule 3).
- Category names change between years, so keep a per-year category
  table instead of a fixed list.
- Add `docs/awards-research.md` sources to the source register in
  `docs/data-sources.md` (CLAUDE.md rule 2). That file belongs to another
  thread, so those entries are noted here for it to pick up.
