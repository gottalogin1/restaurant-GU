{site} is free to use. We pay for it by selling advertising to restaurants and other businesses. This page explains what is paid for and how you can tell.

## How you can tell what is paid

- Every paid placement carries the label **Sponsored**, above or on the ad, on phones and computers alike.
- Sponsored placements sit in their own marked area, apart from the regular list of restaurants.
- Links in ads are marked for search engines as sponsored links.
- A sponsor in our newsletter is labeled "Sponsored" and placed after the regular items.

## What can be bought

- **Sponsored listings and banners** on village, mall, cuisine and home pages.
- **Premium profiles**, which add features to a restaurant's own page, such as more photos and a reservation or ordering button. A premium profile does not move the restaurant up in results or change anything about other restaurants' pages.
- **Newsletter sponsorships.**

## What can never be bought

- Ratings, reviews, award results, or a restaurant's place in our regular results. A restaurant that advertises appears in its normal position in the results, with no boost.
- The removal or hiding of a review.
- Owner quotes, editor's notes and replies to reviews. These are free and never part of a package.
- Use of an awards program's name, logo or badge in an ad.

## Content from restaurants

Text and photos that a restaurant supplies are shown under "From the owner" so you know who wrote them.

## Commission links

We do not currently use links that pay us a commission. If we add any, they will be labeled next to the link.
