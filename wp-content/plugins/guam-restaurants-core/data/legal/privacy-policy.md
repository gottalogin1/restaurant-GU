## What we collect and why

### When you visit

- **Visit statistics.** Our own analytics count page views without cookies and without keeping your full IP address. They respect your browser's Do Not Track setting. The data stays on our server.
- **Restaurant and ad counts.** When you view a restaurant page or see or click an ad, we add one to a daily total. We store only the totals: no IP address, cookie, account or browser details.
- **Server logs.** Our host keeps standard web server logs (IP address, browser, page requested, time) to keep the site secure and fix problems, for [HOST LOG RETENTION].
- **Saved restaurants without an account.** When you tap the heart on a restaurant, the restaurant is added to a list kept in your own browser (its local storage, not a cookie). We do not receive that list. To show your Saved page, your browser sends us only the restaurant numbers, the same as opening those restaurant pages. Clearing your browser's site data deletes the list.
- **Shared lists.** A shared list link contains only restaurant numbers. It says nothing about you, and we do not record who shares or opens it.

### When you create an account

You never need an account to use the site or to save restaurants. An account keeps your saved list on our server so you can see it on any device.

- **Signing up with email.** Your username, email address and password. WordPress stores the password only as a one-way hash.
- **Signing in with Google or Facebook.** You sign in on Google's or Facebook's own page. They send us your name, email address, profile picture and an account number for this site, which we keep with your account. We do not get your password, contacts, friends or posts, and we never post anything for you. Google's and Meta's privacy policies cover what you give them ([Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Meta](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/)).
- **Your saved list.** The restaurants you saved. If you saved some in your browser before signing in, they are added to your account when you sign in and removed from the browser.
- **What we do not do with it.** We use your saved list only to show it to you and in links you choose to share. We do not use it for advertising, we do not show other people how many times a restaurant was saved, and we do not make personalized recommendations.
- **Deleting your account.** Sign in, open the Saved page and choose Delete my account. Your account, your saved list and the details Google or Facebook sent us are deleted at once. If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove this site under Settings > Apps and Websites in Facebook and then delete your account here. Restaurant owners and staff write to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] instead, because their accounts are linked to listings, orders or moderation records.

### When you send us something

- **Reviews.** Your rating, review text, display name, and any connection to the restaurant you tell us about. A person checks each review before it is published with your display name. If you are not signed in, we email you a link to confirm the review; we use your email address only to send that link and do not keep it.
- **Corrections and listing requests.** Your name, email address and request. We keep them in a private queue so we can check the facts, reply to you and record what we changed.
- **Advertising inquiries.** Your name, business, email address, phone number, budget and message, so we can reply and follow up.
- **Restaurant owner accounts and claims.** Your name, email address and the restaurant you claim. To confirm you work there we email a code to an address at the restaurant's own website domain.
- **Premium profile purchases.** Our shop (WooCommerce) keeps the order details you enter, such as your name, billing email and address. Payment is made through PayPal; we never see or store card numbers. PayPal's own privacy statement applies to what you give PayPal.
- **Newsletter.** If you subscribe, SendFox keeps your email address and sends the newsletter for us. Every issue has an unsubscribe link.

### Spam and abuse limits

When you send a form we keep a one-way hash of your IP address and email address for up to one day, so one person cannot flood the site. The hash cannot be turned back into the address.

## Content from other services

- **Maps.** Your browser loads map images from OpenFreeMap, which receives your IP address and browser details like any website you visit.
- **Google Maps.** Some restaurant pages show an embedded Google map. Google receives your IP address and may set cookies in that map ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
- **Ratings.** Ratings from Google, Yelp and Foursquare are fetched by our server. Your browser does not contact those services unless you follow a link to them.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies the site needs to work. Saved restaurants are kept in your browser's local storage, which, like these cookies, is used only for something you asked for. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and our analytics set no cookies, so we do not show a cookie banner. If that changes we will ask before setting any other cookie.

| Cookie | Who sets it | When | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| wordpress_logged_in_*, wordpress_sec_*, wordpress_test_cookie | This site | Only when you sign in | Keeps you signed in |
| lg_redirect_to, PHPSESSID | This site | Only while you sign in with Google or Facebook (up to 20 minutes) | Brings you back to the page you were on and protects the sign-in |
| Google or Facebook cookies | Google or Meta | Only on their sign-in page, if you choose to use it | Set by Google or Meta for their sign-in |
| woocommerce_cart_hash, woocommerce_items_in_cart, wp_woocommerce_session_* | This site | Only when you buy a premium profile | Keeps your cart |
| PayPal cookies | PayPal | Only at checkout | Payment and fraud prevention |
| Google cookies | Google | Only on pages with an embedded Google map | Set by Google inside the map |
