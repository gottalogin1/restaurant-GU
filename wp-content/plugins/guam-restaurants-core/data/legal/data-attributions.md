{site} is built from open data, public records, information from restaurant owners, and facts our editors check against a published source. This page lists where the data comes from and the license it is used under.

## Restaurant details

- **OpenStreetMap.** Restaurant names, addresses, phone numbers, websites, hours and locations come in part from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). Corrections made on OpenStreetMap reach this site at the next weekly import.
- **Restaurant owners.** Verified owners can update their own listing's hours, menu links, photos and specials. Their text and photos are shown under "From the owner".
- **Our editors.** Facts we add by hand are checked against a published source, such as the restaurant's own website or a government record.

## Maps and places

- **Village boundaries:** [U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html) files (public domain).
- **Map images:** [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/), using the [OpenMapTiles](https://www.openmaptiles.org/) schema, © OpenMapTiles, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Maps are drawn with [MapLibre GL JS](https://maplibre.org/) (BSD license).
- **Google Maps:** some restaurant pages show an embedded Google map, under Google's terms.

## Ratings

Ratings from Google, Yelp and Foursquare are shown next to their source's name, as each service provides them, and link back to that service. They belong to those services and their users; we do not change or combine them. Reviews written on this site are shown separately and are labeled as reviews from our users.

## Awards

Award results are recorded from the results the awarding organization published, with a link to that source. This site is not affiliated with the organizations whose results it lists.
