## Our editorial statement

Advertising on {site} never affects ratings, awards, rankings, or which restaurants we list or remove. Star ratings come from the third-party sources shown beside them and from our users. Awards are recorded from published results, with a link to the original source. Sponsored placements are labeled 'Sponsored' and appear outside the organic order of results. Advertisers cannot review, edit, or remove user reviews, and they cannot pay to change the facts on a listing. We accept corrections from owners and publish them when verified.

## How we keep advertising separate

- The people who sell advertising do not decide or change ratings, rankings, awards, highlights, listings, or review moderation, and editorial staff are not paid commission on ad sales.
- Our advertising contracts say that buying an ad does not affect any of these. Anyone who offers otherwise on our behalf is breaking this policy.
- A restaurant that buys a sponsored placement still appears in its normal position in our regular results, with no boost.
- We never use "Featured," "Top pick," or "Recommended" for a paid placement.
- Awards are never part of an advertising package or sponsor offer, and we do not use awards programs' names, logos, or badges in ads.
- Highlights (owner quotes, editor's notes, and reviews) are free. They cannot be bought and are not part of any package.
- Text or photos supplied by a restaurant are shown under "From the owner."

More detail is on our [advertising disclosure]({disclosure_url}) page.

## Corrections

Anyone can report a factual error through the [correction form]({correction_url}), which every listing links to. Owners and managers use the same form to ask for their listing to be changed or removed. We reply to corrections within 7 days, and to owner requests to change or remove a listing within 14 days.

- We check each fact against a source: the restaurant's own website or signage, a government record, a published award result, or the restaurant's verified owner account.
- Verified corrections are published and appear in the listing's change history with the date and source.
- Closed restaurants are marked "Closed" with the date rather than deleted, so award history stays accurate.
- We do not change ratings, reviews, rankings or award results on request. Award results change only if the publisher corrects its own published results.
- Requests tied to advertising ("I'll advertise if you change this") are declined.

## Sponsored features

Our staff write every sponsored feature in our own words. The advertiser may supply facts, photos and quotes and may check a draft for facts only. Sponsored features carry the label "Sponsored" above the headline and a line saying who paid for them. They never contain star ratings, rankings, comparisons with named competitors, claims to be "the best," or any awards program's name, logo or badge.

## Report a concern

If anyone offers or asks for special treatment in exchange for advertising, tell us through the [correction form]({correction_url}). We investigate every report.
